VISOR

vision training · Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Visor does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

No accounts, no login, no email, no identifiers. No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, no crash reporters. No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Where your data lives

All app data — your training sessions, streak, best score, settings, and an optional Solana wallet association — is stored locally in on-device SQLite. It never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Optional Seed Vault connection

If you choose to connect a Solana wallet via Seed Vault, the connection is established through the on-device Mobile Wallet Adapter. We do not receive, store, or transmit your keys, seed phrase, or wallet contents. A wallet public address is shown only to identify the connection locally.

Third parties

Visor has no third-party data sharing because there is no data to share.

Children

Visor does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact env5150@proton.me.
